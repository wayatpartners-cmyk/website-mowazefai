What is an AI voice agent?

It's a digital employee that answers the phone. When someone calls, the agent greets them, listens to and understands their question even in colloquial Arabic, and replies in a clear voice. It's not a "press 1 for sales" menu; it's a natural conversation that ends with a result: an answer, an appointment, or a transfer to the right person.

Inbound calls

Answers immediately, even when every line is busy or the office is closed.

Booking by voice

Checks the calendar, suggests free slots and confirms the appointment during the call.

Outbound follow-up calls

Appointment reminders, booking confirmations and follow-ups with interested customers.

Missed-call callbacks

If a call is missed, it calls the customer back automatically at the time you set.

Logged in your CRM

Caller name, number and a call summary are written to your system after every call.

Transfer to staff

When the caller wants a person or the case is sensitive, it transfers the call or asks the team to call back.

When do you need a voice agent?

If your customers prefer calling to typing, if busy periods mean missed calls, or if you want to cover after-hours without hiring extra staff, a voice agent solves that. It suits clinics and medical centers, real estate companies, maintenance and service businesses, restaurants that take reservations, and any company that relies on the phone for sales or customer service.

The voice agent doesn't replace your team; it lightens the load. It takes the repetitive, simple calls, and the important or complex ones reach your team with a summary, so your staff know from the first second what the caller needs.

We keep things transparent for callers: the agent introduces itself as your business's automated assistant and offers the option to speak with a person at any time.

AI voice agent FAQ

Does the voice agent speak natural-sounding Arabic?

Yes. We choose a voice and dialect close to your audience (Jordanian, Gulf or Modern Standard Arabic), and it understands callers who speak colloquially. It can speak English too.

Can I use my current business number?

It depends on your phone provider. Usually we can forward calls from your current number to the agent at set times or when the line is busy, and we explain the options in the discovery call.

What if the caller asks for a person?

The agent transfers the call to a staff member if one is available, or records the request and notifies the team to call back, with a call summary.

Can it make outbound calls?

Yes, for appointment reminders, booking confirmations and following up with customers who asked to be contacted, at appropriate and respectful times.

Where are call recordings and summaries stored?

In the systems you choose, such as your CRM. We agree with you from the start what is stored and for how long, and you can request deletion at any time.
