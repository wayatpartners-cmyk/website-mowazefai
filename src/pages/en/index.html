FAQ

Questions we get before we start.

Didn't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply within hours.

How is your bot different from ready-made bots?

Ready-made bots reply with fixed templates. We build an AI bot trained on your business data and rules that understands questions however they're phrased, and connects to your CRM and systems to actually act: log a lead, take an order or book an appointment.

Which CRMs and systems do you integrate with?

HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Salesforce, Odoo, Pipedrive, Google Sheets, Shopify, WooCommerce and Google Calendar, plus any system with an API or webhooks. If you run a custom system, we'll review it with you in the discovery call.

Can the bot give wrong information?

The bot answers from the knowledge base we prepare with you, under clear rules: if it doesn't know or the question is sensitive, it hands the conversation to a human instead of guessing.

How long until it's live?

A basic single-channel bot is ready within days. Multi-channel projects with CRM integrations are scoped after discovery, usually weeks, not months.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on channels, integrations and conversation volume. After the free discovery call we send a clear, itemized quote with no obligation.

Is my customers' data safe?

Your data is yours. We use it only to run your bot, apply access controls and encrypted connections, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.
