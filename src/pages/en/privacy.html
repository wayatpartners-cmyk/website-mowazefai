Privacy policy

In plain words: what we collect, why, and how to reach us about it.

Last updated: November 2026

Data we collect

This website has no sign-up forms and doesn't ask for personal data. When you contact us, it's through:

  • WhatsApp: we see your name, number and the messages you send, and use them only to reply and provide the service you asked for.
  • Email: we use your address and message only to reply to you.

Visit statistics

We use analytics tools (Vercel Web Analytics and Google Analytics) to understand visit counts, the most-viewed pages and how many people tap the WhatsApp button. This information is aggregated and not used to identify you personally.

Storage on your device

The site remembers in your browser whether you have already seen the opening logo animation, so it doesn't repeat during the same visit. The analytics and advertising tools listed here may use cookies.

Meta advertising (Facebook and Instagram)

We may use the Meta Pixel to measure how well our ads on Facebook and Instagram perform. It records page visits and clicks on contact buttons (WhatsApp, booking, email, phone) and shares this information with Meta under the Meta Privacy Policy. You can control the ads you see in your Facebook ad preferences.

Sharing data

We don't sell your data. The only sharing is with the analytics and advertising tools listed above, solely to measure visits and ads.
