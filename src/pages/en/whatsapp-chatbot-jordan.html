A WhatsApp chatbot suits any business in Jordan that receives a lot of messages and wants to reply quickly without adding headcount: real estate companies fielding questions about apartments and prices, institutes and training centers enrolling students, online stores answering order and delivery questions, travel agencies, salons, maintenance and service companies, and clinics.

WhatsApp chatbot FAQ

Can I use my company's existing WhatsApp number?

Usually yes, through the WhatsApp Business API. We review your current number in the discovery call and explain the options and any Meta verification steps before we start.

How is it different from WhatsApp Business auto-replies?

A standard auto-reply sends one fixed message. A Mowazef AI chatbot understands the customer's question however it's phrased, answers from your business information, books and logs in your CRM, and hands over to a person when needed.

Can the bot give a customer wrong information?

The bot answers only from the knowledge base we prepare with you, under clear rules. If it doesn't know the answer, it tells the customer someone from the team will follow up and hands over the chat.

Which CRMs does it work with?

HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Salesforce, Odoo, Pipedrive and Google Sheets, plus any system with an API or webhooks.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on conversation volume and the number of integrations. After the free discovery call we send a clear, itemized quote with no obligation.
