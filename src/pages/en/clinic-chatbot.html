What does a clinic chatbot do?

Most patient messages repeat: "Any appointments tomorrow?", "How much is a cleaning?", "Where are you located?", "I need to move my appointment." These questions take up a lot of the front desk's time, and the bot can handle them accurately and quickly, leaving the team free to focus on the patients in front of them.

Appointment booking

Shows available slots by doctor and service, and confirms the booking in the calendar or clinic system.

Reschedule & cancel

Patients can move or cancel with a message, and the slot opens up for someone else.

Appointment reminders

An automatic reminder before the visit, with the option to confirm or reschedule in the same chat.

Services & hours

Services, doctors, opening hours, location, and accepted insurance if you want to list it.

Medical questions go to people

The bot gives no medical advice. Any medical question is routed to the clinic team.

Organized patient records

Name, number and requested service are saved in your system instead of getting lost in messages.

From first message to confirmed appointment

This is how a typical booking conversation flows, and we tune every step to how your clinic works.

  1. The patient messages

    On WhatsApp or Instagram, at any hour, in colloquial or formal Arabic, or English.

  2. The bot understands

    It asks about the service, preferred doctor and suitable time, with a few clear questions.

  3. The booking is confirmed

    The patient picks from genuinely free slots, and the appointment is written to the calendar or system.

  4. Reminder & follow-up

    A reminder before the visit, the option to reschedule, and a review request afterwards if you like.

Is it right for any clinic?

The chatbot suits dental clinics, dermatology and aesthetics, physiotherapy, women's and children's clinics, and multi-specialty medical centers. The more daily messages, doctors and services you have, the more you gain from organizing bookings automatically.

We connect the bot to Google Calendar or to the booking system your clinic already uses, if it has an API. If the clinic still books in a notebook or spreadsheet, we suggest the simplest way to move appointments to a digital calendar without complicating the team's work.

Patient privacy comes first: the bot doesn't ask for sensitive medical details in chat, doesn't diagnose or give treatment advice, and data is stored only in systems the clinic controls. We review every reply with the clinic before launch.

Clinic chatbot FAQ

Does the bot give medical advice?

No. The bot is for bookings and general clinic information. Any medical question is routed to the clinic team, or the patient is asked to book a consultation.

How does the bot know which slots are free?

It reads availability from the clinic's calendar (such as Google Calendar) or from the booking system if it has an API, so it only offers genuinely free times.

Can it handle several doctors?

Yes. We configure it with your doctors, each doctor's services and working hours; the patient chooses or the bot suggests the best fit.

What if the patient wants to talk to the receptionist?

They can ask for a person at any moment, and the bot hands the chat to the team with a summary of the request.

Do I need to change my current clinic system?

Usually not. We try to connect the bot to your existing system, and if that isn't possible we suggest a simple solution that doesn't disrupt the clinic.
