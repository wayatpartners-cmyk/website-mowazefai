FAQ

Everything you need to know before we start.

Didn't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply within hours.

How much does a website cost in Jordan?

It depends on the number of pages, languages and extras like a chatbot or SEO. Tell us about your business on WhatsApp and we'll send a clear, itemized quote within a day, with no obligation.

How long does it take?

We deliver websites within 7 days of receiving your content and photos. Chatbot and automation timelines depend on the systems we connect, and we set them with you on the first call.

What is SEO, and will my site show on Google?

SEO (search engine optimization) prepares your site to appear in Google when people search for your service. Every site we build is fast, has a title and description per page, structured data and Google Maps integration. Rankings take time and nobody can guarantee #1, but you get the strongest possible start and we keep following up.

What do you need from me?

Your business name and logo if you have one, services, opening hours, address, WhatsApp number and photos if available. If you don't have copy ready, we'll write it for you, professionally and search-optimized.

Who handles hosting and the domain?

We set up hosting and connect the domain. The domain is registered in your name and is yours, and any yearly cost is stated in the quote from the start.

Can I make changes after launch?

Of course. Small edits after launch are quick, just message us on WhatsApp. Larger changes or new pages are agreed clearly together.
