Questions we hear a lot

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of the website and the services you need. Tell us about your clinic or business on WhatsApp and we'll send a clear, itemized quote with no commitment.

How long does a website take?

We deliver websites within 7 days of receiving your information and photos. Chatbot and automation timelines depend on the systems we connect, and we agree on them in the first call.

What do you need from me to start?

Your clinic name and logo if you have one, a list of services, opening hours, address and WhatsApp number, and photos of the clinic and team if available. No content ready? We'll help you write it.

Who handles hosting and the domain?

We set up hosting and connect the domain. The domain is registered in your name and belongs to you, and any yearly costs are clearly listed in the quote from day one.

What if I want changes after launch?

Of course. Small edits after launch are quick, just message us on WhatsApp. Larger changes or new pages are agreed on clearly together.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every website we build is search-ready from the ground up: fast loading, a title and description for every page, structured data, and a Google Maps connection. Rankings take time and nobody can guarantee the top spot, but you get the strongest possible start.
