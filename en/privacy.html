Privacy policy

In plain words: what we collect, why, and how to reach us about it.

Last updated: October 2026

Data we collect

This website has no sign-up forms and doesn't ask for personal data. When you contact us, it's through:

  • WhatsApp: we see your name, number and the messages you send, and use them only to reply and provide the service you asked for.
  • Email: we use your address and message only to reply to you.

Visit statistics

We use analytics tools (Vercel Web Analytics and Google Analytics) to understand visit counts, the most-viewed pages and how many people tap the WhatsApp button. This information is aggregated and not used to identify you personally.

Storage on your device

The site saves your light or dark mode choice in your browser only. It never reaches us.

Sharing data

We don't sell your data or share it with anyone for marketing purposes.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you or delete any conversation or data we hold about you at any time. Email info@mowazefai.com.
